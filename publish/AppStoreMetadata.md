Long description:

Folioseam is a bindery bench for restoration and new bindings. Open a project, track signatures and sewing progress, log material lots with cost, attach optional condition photos, and export a project package when the job leaves the bench.

Inside the app:
• Exploded binding cross-section that mirrors sew progress
• Sections with page counts and sewn flags
• Material hub with lots, units, and spend
• Stage tasks for forwarding, covering, and finishing
• JSON / PDF export from the export hub
